## 1. Core Unit Economics Metrics

$$\text{Unit Cost} = \frac{\text{Total Attributed Cloud Spend}}{\text{Total Business Operation Volume}}$$

| Metric Name | Unit Cost | Monthly Business Volume | Attributed Spend | Efficiency Status |

## 2. Business Value Correlation

- **Spend vs Revenue**: Cloud spend scales sub-linearly relative to revenue ($+12\%$ spend vs $+38\%$ transaction volume increase).
- **Correlation Caution**: High correlation between traffic and infrastructure cost does not imply inefficiency; unit economic ratios track actual operational leverage.
